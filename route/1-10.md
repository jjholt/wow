# 1 - 10: Zephras Isle
## 1 - 4
grind to lvl3 + 300xp on humanoids. Minimum lvl3 + 860xp when heading to hand quests back.

buy 2 leatherworking reagent bag and a knife.

Read ley-line during grind. refresh leyline on quest
## 4 - 6
bears then al'alketh neophytes. min lvl5 + 2000xp before hand-in of al'alketh quest.
kill galestriders on the way. if tornado is up, pick it.
do sit quest and stretch legs for 1min. pick both cooking and skinning.

* make sure you have 5s for 6slot bag*. Vendor leather if you must, then buy back.
## 6 - 8
Grind prideclaws until ~2min of leyline. head to windshapers for refresh and grind any prideclaws/galestriders.
Try to walk on air towards a matriarch. stay in one location for all protectors then make way out for youths.
After bridge there are more galestriders. try to get 13 stride meat, but kill any galestriders you see on the way.

Min lvl 7 + 1615 xp when going back to hand in quests and 60 scrap of leather for LW quest.
Buy rest of bags. must have 2s left over for after training LW/buying bags for entangling roots.

Learn fishing and do it to 20.

easy vulgara solo. Eat and go for pilfered windstone/bandit.

## 8 - 10 (Before Valanaar)
refresh leyline during bandits, then refresh again before Among the Faithful (while stormcallers are friendly).

Refresh food buff and go for stormcallers. Refresh leyline when done -- should have maybe 4min left of it.

[!TIP]
Hearthstone after killing Skypriest Aanders

Refresh food buff and grind towards Valanaar. galestriders are a good source of leather.
Reach around lvl 9 + 3000 xp before you enter the city. 

### Valanar
Checklist as you enter the city (in order):
- Check AH or buy zephrali greatstaff. buy/trade for weightstone, minor force, minor strength
- Train skinning

Hand in all quests, and do Bugged while moving around. Start fishing until you have enough food for 50 cooking or tornado spawns. They last 2min45s.

Head to Fillion Flamebreeze quest *only* with speed buff. 

## 10
Go back to fishing and cooking until 50 cooking. 
Cook all the Brilliant Smallfish and the Longjaw Mud Snapper. Vendor/AH Brilliant smallfish.

Only head out to bear quest once you have a speed buff.

[!TIP]
If hearthstone is available after the bear quest, use it. Otherwise run back. There's a chance for tornado near bear.

Head back into the city, clear bags, learn new skills, level up professions while waiting for tornado. Grab a tornado and head out.

Matriarch => Blood tithe.
