# 20-30 Kalimdor awaits
## 20-21 Deadmines
Possibly worth doing all quests. In any case, should be 21 by the end. Hand in all quests in SW, get weapon enchanted.

fly to Lakeshire if necessary, then portal to Moonglade

## 21-22 Auberdine
[!IMPORTANT]
Set hearthstone to Auberdine

Pick up Fruit of the Sea and leave for a north loop. Pick up Gyromast's retrieval before farming the crabs for fruit of the sea.

Grind 20 relics from the Naga. 
Hearthstone back and run down to Onu/Lost Master

## 22-23 Ashenvale, Stonetalon mountain
If glowing shard is busy, don't bother.

[!IMPORTANT]
Set hearthstone to Astranaar

If 22, run to river 63,71 for amulet. then run back to astranaar, clear bags and go to stonetalon

Grind Venture Co until all quests are done. If a lot of horde, just snipe the right mobs.

Head north, pick up Stonetalon Supply run at 50,40. Grind pridewings until bags full ~30 min, then go to Stonetalon Peak and fly back to Astranaar.
[!NOTE]
Might be worth staying until lvl 24 here. do a run to clear bags in Stonetalon Peak.

### Leatherworking break
The pridewing farm should have given about 50 medium leather, 60 light leather, a few pristine leather and a few hide.

Depending on how close to LW 100 you are, fly to Darnassus to buy the remaining supplies and restock consumables.

Make:
* Dark Leather Pants

Check AH for:
* Dark Leather Shoulders
* Medium hide. if it's cheap, dark leather belt could be worth it.

[!IMPORTANT]
Pick up BFD quests in Darnassus

Head to Auberdine => Menethil Harbour

## 23-24 Wetlands => Ironforge

Head East. No reason to fight crocolisks close to harbour.
Skip the Crude Flint quest for now. 

Do all the quests that aren't too difficult/overly farmed, ding 24. Go to Ironforge
Need:
* 4 minor mana potion
* 2 minor fortitude elixir

[!IMPORTANT]
Pick up BFD quest north of the city

## 24-25 Blackfathom Deeps
Kill Ruuzel while waiting.
