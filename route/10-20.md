# 10-20 Leaving Zephras Isle, Darkshore and dungeons.
## 10-14 Zephras Isle
Keep working through the blood tithe quest. Should reach lvl 11 during highway bandits.

Once you finish the chain, Hearthstone. go to trainer, clear bags, craft:
* light armour kit to 45
* 1x each: light leather bracers, embossed leather cloak
* embossed leather gloves to 55.
* at 55: make fine leather belt
* Only fighting mobs that drop light hide at lvl16+, so just accumulate more leather.

buy consumables, then head northwest for Catching Wind. make sure you have a speed buff. interrupt whatever you're doing if the speedbuff is up.
Only bother with Tower Defence/avenged tenfold if you're ahead. Otherwise head north for the rep grind.

if doing tower defence, should reach lvl 12.5 by the end. if so, valanaar to train. There's a lot of back and forth and some dialogue. good time for tea.

Quests at the north should take you to lvl 14. Skip Making Our Move until you have finished all the quests up north. Not worth farming ears now as rep has been nerfed to 200/5ears.

## 14-16 Stormwind, Ironforge, Darkshore
Head to stormwind and grab portal to moonglade. Grab Business in Auberdine, hand in Welcome to Azeroth.
Take tram to ironforge:
* Speak to Magni and Mekkatorque
* Set hearthstone to ironforge
* head to loch modan to pick up Hall of Thanes quest: Underground Map. hand in quest, then portal.
Portal to moonglade => Darnassus FP, Tyrande => Auberdine. Gather all quests:

Leave through the beach. 

* tag crabs
* washed ashore
* Keep moving south and tagging crabs
* Do fulborg camp/Rabid bear
* Pick up fall of ameth'aran => kill the undead. Group up for Jai'vhanel and anaya.
* Hand in fall of ameth'aran => The red crystal => Auberdine

Second loop. Leave through the docks:

* Washed ashore
* Threshers
* Back to auberdine to unlock all the washed ashore.
Third loop. Leave through north. 

* Walk the coast to grab beached sea creature.
* Buzzbox handin => Murloc quest
* walk in land for moonstalker runts
* beached sea creature x2 => Cliffspring River
* Kill moonstalkers/rabid bears. likely lvl 16.
* If lvl16, just abandon bashal'aran.
Return to Auberdine:

* Water Cascades, while hunting moonstalkers.
* walk to beach north of murkdeep. pick up beached sea creature x2
* try to finish rabid bears and moonstalkers on the way to fulborgs.
* Auberdine => fill moonwell water => Buzzbox => moonglade

## 16-18 Hall of Thanes, Westfall
Should be close to 18 when done with HoT.

Fly to Stormwind, pick up all the DM quests. spawn rate of Oh Brother and Collecting Memories is better, so might be worth doing.

Buy consumables. Check AH for waylaid crate: Any green that costs <2g for everything is fine. If available:
* Brawler helmet
* Brawler boots
both bottom right 2nd page.

> [!IMPORTANT]
Set HS to Stormwind

Run to Westfall to start Defias Brotherhood quest.

Pick up Darkshire FP on way to Lakeshire. The back-and-forth is a good time for tea.

Humble Compass and Trial of the Sea Lion are done after the escort, not messenger!

## 18-20 Darkshore
Portal to Moonglade => Auberdine. North Loop:
* Blackwood Corrupted
* Den Mother
* Cave Mushroom
* Principal Source
* Tower of Althalax

Portal to Moonglade, finish aquatic form in Darnassus. Swing by LW trainer if you have light hide and leather. Make: 
* Dark Leather Boots: 24 leather
* Light Leather Pants: 10 leather

> [!IMPORTANT]
Buy bags 2x 8 slot bag or 10 slot

### South Loop
Kill beasts on the way to Onu. If escort quest is up, do it first and then head over to murkdeep/beached monsters.

From southern-most beached monster to Sleeper has Awakened hand-in it takes almost 6min. Don't bother running to Onu, just go to Ashenvale.

Head back up, do Raene's cleansing (HARD!!!) and Zoram Strand. 
> [!NOTE]
Too low level for Ruuzel (min 20). Don't bother handing in statuette before finishing zoram strand.

Head to Onu. Finish Grizzled bears and hand-in. Progress moonstalker.

There is 5700xp in hand-ins, which is roughly lvl 19 + 15700 xp.

Walk to Auberdine and ding 20.
## 20 Cat form
Portal to Moonglade, fly to Darnassus. Get cat quest.

Swing by AH and check for waylaid crate, pristine leather (x4) consumables and bags.

It's likely at this point you've either dropped or found a waylaid crate that is worth buying. if white, the only recipe we get is the helmet.
Leatherworking is unlikely to be high enough, but we won't have another convenient opportunity to buy it. make sure you have:
* 4 pristine leather
* 4 shadowgem
* 2 fine leather
Optional:
* 4+ Salt
